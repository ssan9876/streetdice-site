Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026. Applies to the Street Dice app for iPhone.

Your game stays on your device. Street Dice has no account and no servers of its own, and we never receive your progress. The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information from your device to show and measure those ads. This page explains what, and how to limit it.

What the app stores on your device

Your street name, how your character looks, your play money, gear, dice, progress, turf and settings. It stays inside the app on your iPhone. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, it's included in that backup like other app data. Those backups are managed by Apple or by you, and we can't access them.

Ads from Google AdMob

Street Dice uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show two kinds of ads: optional ads you choose to watch for an in-game reward, and occasional full-screen ads between games. When it loads and shows ads, the SDK collects:

  • Device identifiers. Apple's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. Otherwise, an identifier that's specific to the app.
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address. Not your precise location: Street Dice never asks for it.
  • Advertising data and product interaction, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data from the SDK.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalize them. Google handles it under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We don't receive this information in a form that identifies you, and we don't sell or share anything ourselves.

Your choices

  • Tracking. iOS asks whether Street Dice may track you across other companies' apps and websites. The game works the same either way. You can change your answer any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states. Where the law requires it, Google shows a message asking how your data may be used for ads. You can change that choice later in Street Dice under Settings > Privacy Choices.
  • Fewer ads. Ads with a reward only play when you tap to watch one. The Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads between games.

Purchases

The one purchase in Street Dice, Remove Ads, goes through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we don't receive your payment details or your Apple Account information. The app checks with the App Store on your device whether you own it. Play money is never for sale.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone, for example when a hustler is ready for a rematch or your turf is slipping. They're created on your device, and nothing passes through a server. You can turn them off any time in the iOS Settings app.

Deleting your data

Settings > Reset All Progress in Street Dice erases your game on this device, and deleting the app removes everything it stores. To limit what Google keeps about ads, turn off tracking as described above, or visit My Ad Center.

Age

Street Dice features simulated gambling with play money and is meant for adults 18 and over. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply. Our support mailbox is hosted by Gmail. We keep messages only as long as we need them to help you. If you'd like a message deleted, just ask: streetdicesupport@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
