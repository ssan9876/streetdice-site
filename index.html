Roll the dice. Ten cities. One crown.

Roll against a cast of cartoon hustlers from a Brooklyn stoop to a Hollywood Hills mansion. Beat each state's boss, take over the turf, and hold it.

7 or 11 wins on the come-out. 2, 3 or 12 loses. Anything else is your point.

What's in it

Classic street dice rules. Best-of-3 series: you shoot two rounds, they shoot one, and you see your real chance of winning before every match.

A coast-to-coast road trip. Ten cities from Brooklyn to Los Angeles on a cartoon map, with forty hustlers and ten bosses, each with their own look, trash talk and heat. Beat a boss to take their city.

Turf to hold. States you run pay tribute by the hour, and rival crews try to win them back while you're away.

Your look, your dice. Choose your hair, beard and skin tone at the Barber, dress up with gear that does more than look good, and roll with dice skins you buy or earn.

Something new every day. A daily stash, three fresh hustles, and Street Cred for big moments.

Free to roll

Every match is free to enter. Each round you win pays the hustler's purse, and losing a round never costs you anything. Cash only exists in the game, and you can't buy it.
